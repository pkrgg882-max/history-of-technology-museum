Welcome

Technology is the story of human problem-solving.

Technology did not begin with computers or robots. It began when early humans created tools to solve problems more effectively.

Over thousands of years, humans developed new ways to make food, travel, communicate, build cities, produce energy and share information.

The pace of change accelerated dramatically during the Industrial Revolution and accelerated again during the digital age.

"Every technology tells a story about the problems humans wanted to solve."

About This Project

The Museum

The History of Technology Museum is a virtual educational exhibition showing how humans have developed tools, machines and information systems throughout history.

The timeline is simplified for educational purposes. Dates for very early technologies can vary depending on archaeological evidence and definitions of an invention.
